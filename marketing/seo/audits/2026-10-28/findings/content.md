Score: **40/100** (weight 23%) · Audit date: 2026-10-28

## What works

- Strong first-hand experience signal: the founder story (built for his wife's studio, Brows By Lexxi) is on the homepage.
- /resultados publishes real client numbers (ad spend, leads, cost per lead, bookings): unique data nobody else has.
- Clear offer, price and guarantee in plain Spanish.

## Findings

### [Critical] No informational content: the site can only rank for its own name

**Evidence:** Every indexable page is a sales or legal page. Spanish-language searches such as 'cómo conseguir clientas de micropigmentación', 'cuánto cobrar por cejas ombré en Estados Unidos' and 'anuncios de Facebook para micropigmentadoras' return Spain-focused or English pages; no one answers them for US-based Spanish-speaking artists.

**Fix:** Build a Spanish resource hub (/guias/) with pillar pages and supporting articles (see ACTION-PLAN.md content map).

### [High] No About page and no author entity

**Evidence:** Adriano appears only as a one-line signature. There is no About page, photo, credentials (years selling advertising to local businesses), or link to Brows By Lexxi.

**Fix:** Create /nosotros with Adriano and Lexxi's story, photos, experience and links; byline every article to Adriano with a Person entity.

### [Info] Results page names clients and their ad spend

**Evidence:** Client studio names and spend are public. Good for trust and citability, but needs each client's OK.

**Fix:** Confirm permission; add dates and a short methodology line so AI answers can quote it accurately.
