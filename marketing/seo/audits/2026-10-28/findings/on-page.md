# On-Page SEO — pmuacademia.com

Score: **62/100** (weight 20%) · Audit date: 2026-10-28

## What works

- Titles 36-64 characters and unique; meta descriptions 105-160 characters and unique.
- Open Graph and Twitter cards on every page, with 1200x630 images.
- One H1 per page on the main site.

## Findings

### [Medium] Titles target the brand, not what artists search

**Evidence:** Homepage title 'PMU Academia | Marketing para artistas de micropigmentación' is good; /book ('Agenda tu llamada · PMU Academia USA') and /resultados target no query.

**Fix:** Retitle /resultados as 'Cuánto cuesta conseguir clientas de micropigmentación con anuncios (datos reales)'.

### [Medium] Thin internal linking

**Evidence:** Raw-HTML nav links only to /resultados, /privacidad, /terminos. Homepage does not link to /book in raw HTML.

**Fix:** Add a real nav and footer (Inicio, Resultados, Guías, Nosotros, Agenda tu llamada) rendered in static HTML.
